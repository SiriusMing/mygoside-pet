MyGoSide is a fully open-source, non-commercial project designed to bring gentle emotional support and practical self-management tools to university students. Whether you’re facing exam anxiety, feeling lonely, or simply need a friendly nudge to stay organized, MyGoSide is here to accompany you every step of the way. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## 🎯 Target Audience

- **University students** looking to relieve academic stress or emotional struggles  
- **Counselors and mental-health practitioners** who want an AI-powered tool for outreach  
- **Anyone** seeking a warm, on-demand digital companion for mood tracking and goal reminders :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Interactive Virtual Characters**  
   - Clickable 8-bit style “pet” avatars that greet you, offer encouragement, and adapt to time of day or holidays :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}  
2. **AI-Powered Chat**  
   - Engage in empathetic text & voice conversations to share your thoughts, get suggestions, or simply lighten your mood :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}  
3. **Mood Tracking & Analytics**  
   - Record daily emotions with tags, notes, and view your history via pie charts and bar graphs to spot trends over time :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}  
4. **Life Reminders & Achievements**  
   - Schedule tasks, anniversaries, or study sessions; unlock “stars” for each completed action and redeem exclusive character artwork :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}  
5. **Character & Costume Customization**  
   - Switch between multiple characters, each with a unique personality, and dress them up in a variety of outfits :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}
